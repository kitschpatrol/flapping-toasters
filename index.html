This is a port of the original 2011 version of Flapping Toasters for the web. It’s an interactive homage to After Dark’s Flying Toasters screensaver.

To start, allow access to your webcam and take a few steps back so that your upper body is visible to the camera.

Then, flap your arms to fly higher, clap to release toast, and tilt your arms to do a barrel roll.
